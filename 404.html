The “Open in App” button does nothing. What now?

Confirm the app is installed, then tap the button again. Telegram, Instagram and Facebook use built-in browsers that can block app links: tap the ⋮ menu and choose “Open in browser”, then tap “Open in App” again.

What can I find inside Cooked Lama?

Movies, web series, anime, cartoons, online games, smart search, an AI assistant, market tools, rewards, referrals, watch history, Continue Watching and Premium options.

Is Cooked Lama free?

You can explore the app for free. Premium plans may unlock extra features, and plan details, pricing and benefits are shown in the app.

How do rewards and referrals work?

Eligible activities and invitations may earn rewards. Availability and conditions vary by eligibility and program rules, and they are explained in the app and on the Telegram channel.

Are the market tools financial advice?

No. Market tools are for informational and educational purposes only. Always do your own research.

Which devices are supported?

Cooked Lama is built for Android. The website works everywhere, but opening the app needs an Android phone with the app installed.

How do I delete my data or get support?

Use “Delete All Lama Data” in the app’s policy section, or message @CookedLamaBot on Telegram with your request.
